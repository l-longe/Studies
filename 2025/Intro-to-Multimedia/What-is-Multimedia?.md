## The ancient definition would be...
- Using more than one medium of expression, communication, etc.
- The provision of audio and video material cross-referenced to a computer text.

- Multimedia must incorporate:
	- text
	- sound
	- still graphic images
	- motion graphics
	- animation
	- and interaction!

## Multimedia Computers
They serve two primary functions:
- Development tools for the creation of multimedia products
	- e.g. unity, blender, apple, unreal engine
- Delivery devices for multimedia applications
	- browsers of player applications like steam?
## Multimedia Applications
### Previous
- Computer-assisted instruction (CAI)
- Computer-assisted learning (CAL)
- Computer-based instruction (CBI) etc...

### Present
- instruction
- entertainment
- scientific analysis, research, and presentations
- business applications, advertising, and sales
- art and other humanities purposes
- adaptive technologies for the disabled (used to only mean turning text into sound and sound into text)
