# Electromagnetic Spectrum
radio-UHF/VHF/TV-micro-infrared-visible-ultraviolet-xrays-gamma rays (these are the parts of the electromagnetic spectrum)
- They go from low energy to high energy
- Visible light is 400-700 nanometres
- The sky is blue because visible light from the sun gets scattered.
# Speed of light
- The speed of loght is *3 million m/s*
- This is calculated by using frequency multiplied by wavelength
# Power of light
- This is measured in several different ways:
	- radiance (watts)
	- luminance (lumens)
	- subjective brightness
# Light receptors
There are two types of light receptors:
- Rods
	- black and white
	- only work in low light
	- 120 million rods in each eye
- Cones
	- colour sensors
	- only 6-7 cones in each eye
	- only work in bright light
- Humans are trichromatic, meaning that they see light through combinations of three main colours. These are
	- Red
	- Green
	- Blue
- RGB's inverted are CMY (cyan, maroon, yellow)
# Colour Gamut
